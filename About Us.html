A family for children who have lost theirs

Behind every statistic is a child with a name, a laugh and a dream. Many of the children we welcome arrive with nothing but a small bag and a lot of questions.

We give them a bed, nourishing food, a classroom and, most of all, someone who believes in them. Our goal is simple: that no child grows up feeling alone.

Arlette Munga
Founder and Coordinator
